# The Collective Designs — Dark Little Worlds

Commission/portfolio site for The Collective Designs. No store, cart, checkout, Flappy Bat/Moth or prize system.

## Edit the site
- `index.html` — all visible wording, services, prices and policies.
- `styles.css` — visual styling.
- `script.js` — navigation, reveal animations and commission email form.
- `assets/caitlin-chibi.png` — avatar artwork.

## Email
Commission enquiries open the visitor's mail app addressed to:
`thecollective.inkanddesign@gmail.com`

## Design direction
This version deliberately mixes the mood of the old “Dark Little Worlds” Night Garden site with the newer commission-focused site: atmospheric, gothic and personal, but restrained enough to remain readable and easy to navigate.
