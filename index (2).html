About me ♡

Hey, I'm Caitlin (or just Cat).

I'm a digital artist, tattoo enthusiast and creative soul based in South Africa.

I started The Collective Designs because I wanted a space where art, nature, and the darker, weirder parts of my brain could exist together.

I've always been drawn to the moon, moths, plants, stories and strange little details — and I love turning those obsessions into art, designs and meaningful pieces.

When I'm not drawing or designing, you'll probably find me with my headphones in, overthinking something, drinking too much coffee, or hanging out with my cat.

I'm here to create, to connect, and to bring your ideas to life.

THE BORING BUT IMPORTANT BIT

Fees, policies &
tiny-print things.

🧾 Revisions

2 rounds of revisions included. Additional revisions are charged separately. Major changes after approval are quoted separately.

⚡ Rush fees

5–7 days: normal · 3–4 days: +15% · 1–2 days: +30% · same day: +50–100%

💳 Payment

50% deposit to begin and 50% before final files. Projects above R10,000 may use 40% deposit → 30% midway → 30% completion.

📦 Source files

Finished exported files are included. Editable/source files are an additional deliverable at +20–30%.

📜 Commercial rights

Personal use included. Small-business commercial use +25%. Full commercial ownership/licensing +50–100%. Exclusive rights: custom quote.

🖨️ Printing

Design fees are separate from printing costs. You pay the design fee, then the actual printing cost.
