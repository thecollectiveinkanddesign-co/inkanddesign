02 · ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Caitlin.
Or just Cat.

I'm a digital artist, tattoo enthusiast and creative soul based in South Africa.

I started The Collective Designs because I wanted a space where art, nature and the darker, weirder parts of my brain could exist together.

When I'm not drawing or designing, you'll probably find me with headphones in, overthinking something, drinking too much coffee or hanging out with my cat.

I'm here to create, connect, and turn your ideas into something you can actually see.
